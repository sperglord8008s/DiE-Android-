RHash 1.4.5 MD4 implementation and byte-order support (unmodified source)
Source: https://github.com/rhash/RHash/tree/6ea6791024499524cf41f9e0b6b08d456bf4a5e2/librhash
Exact per-file SHA-256 values are in hash-engine/source-lock.json.
The other whole-file digests use the already pinned MIT xxfclib at 0fc8b4fa12c2c9c97d79fa3a3806667fc1da9b9d.
MD4 is supplied only for legacy content identification, not security.
