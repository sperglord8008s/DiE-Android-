CDiE, xxfclib, the Detect-It-Easy signature database and knowledge sources retain
their original licenses. Their pinned revisions and available upstream notices
are bundled under assets/notices and assets/knowledge. AOSP apksig 8.9.2 is
Apache-2.0; see apksig-8.9.2.txt. Imported custom rules retain the license notices
provided by their source; import does not grant redistribution rights.

Google smali/dexlib2 and baksmali 3.0.10 provide DEX interpretation and smali
formatting. Their complete upstream licensing document is smali-3.0.10.txt;
transitive dependency notices are in dex-dependencies.txt. Maven artifact/source
checksums are recorded in scripts/dex-dependencies.sha256 and dex-sources.sha256
in the corresponding source. analysis-engines.properties records the versions.
